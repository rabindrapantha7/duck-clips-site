Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Duck Clips is a private, single-user tool. It only handles data from the owner's own TikTok account.

What it collects

  • The owner's TikTok authorization tokens (access and refresh), received through Login Kit.
  • Basic profile info and the view, like, comment and share counts of videos the tool uploaded, read with the user.info.basic and video.list scopes.

How it is used and stored

Tokens and counts are used only to upload approved videos and show the owner how they performed. They are stored on the owner's own computer, not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising. Revoking the app's access in TikTok settings makes the tokens useless.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect data about any other TikTok user.

Deletion

The owner can delete the stored tokens at any time and revoke access in TikTok's app settings.
